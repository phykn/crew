## Concept and mapping

The existing renderer uses a 16 × 26 base character canvas and independently combines head, upper body, and lower body. This candidate explores a replacement design; it is not required to fit that old canvas. Final display size, rig partitions, canvas, and anchor will be settled after visual approval. Ground anchor is the midpoint between boot soles.
