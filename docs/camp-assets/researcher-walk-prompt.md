# Researcher — Walk loop

## Layout

6 frames, 3 columns × 2 rows, chronological row-major order. Proposed logical cell 64 × 64; actual export may differ. Master faces lower-left; opposite direction is a later horizontal flip.

## Generation prompt

SIX frames arranged in a 3-column by 2-row equal-cell grid, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Same full-body researcher in every cell, continuously facing lower-left; walking IN PLACE without translating across the cell. Orb secured in belt cradle throughout; empty hands swing subtly. Frame 1 near leg forward contact, far leg back, opposite arm forward. Frame 2 weight settles over near leg, both knees soft, torso one pixel lower. Frame 3 legs pass under hips, far foot lifted slightly. Frame 4 far leg forward contact, near leg back, reverse arm swing. Frame 5 weight settles over far leg, torso one pixel lower. Frame 6 near leg passes under hips toward frame 1. Tiny natural mantle/hair follow-through, stable head volume and expression. Visible alternating leg and arm positions, not six nearly identical standing copies. No sprinting, large hops, sliding forward, motion lines, directional arrows or changing camera.

REFERENCE AND IDENTITY LOCK
Image 1 is the exact identity reference: same cute brown-haired adult divination mage, reduced three-head body proportions, rounded highlighted brown eyes, small smile, warm cream tunic, sage open vest, short ochre mantle and clasp, cocoa trousers, chunky brown boots, amber crystal orb and belt cradle. Preserve hair shape, face, head size, outfit cuts, palette, orb size, and pixel density across ALL frames. Do not add a hat, staff, book, bag, weapon, extra cloak or duplicate orb. Changes are motion only. The reference's external brown halo is a defect; remove it rather than copying it.

FACING AND ANCHOR
Every frame keeps the SAME elevated front-three-quarter orientation facing screen lower-left. Never turn to a pure profile, mirror halfway through, or face front in one frame. Future right-facing motion reuses a horizontal flip; no text or asymmetric readable symbols. Use one consistent logical pixel scale, body height, camera and framing. A conceptual cell is 64x64 logical pixels, figure standing about 54 high; feet anchor at local (32,58). Never auto-center each pose or change zoom. For grounded poses planted soles meet the same baseline; airborne walking contact follows the specified tiny stride offsets only. Keep margin above hair and around hands in every cell.

SPRITE CRAFT AND TRANSPARENCY
Hand-authored pixel-art appearance with crisp stair-step edges, deliberate color clusters, limited coordinated shades and neutral upper-left light. Keep expression cute and materials readable. No portrait-like smooth rendering, motion blur, noise, photorealism, glow, outside haze, soft brown halo, cast shadow, floor, pedestal, backdrop, labels, frame borders, numbers, grid lines, watermark, characters other than the one repeated for frames, or equipment not in the reference. Actual alpha outside the character and inside limb gaps. Physical orb is part of the character when present, but all luminous rings, rays, particles and magical emissions are excluded for a separate effect overlay. Every complete figure inside its own cell. No frame crossing cell boundaries.
