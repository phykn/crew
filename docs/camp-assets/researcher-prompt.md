# Researcher Diviner — Reduced-head Pilot

## Scope

Create one researcher pilot plus six agreed motion sheets and a separate casting-effect sheet. Left-facing front three-quarter art is the master; the future renderer may flip it for right-facing. This task only creates candidate assets. Existing code is unchanged.

## Generation prompt

Use case: identity-preserve character redesign.
Create ONE revised full-body standing sprite of the cute brown-haired adventurer in the reference. Preserve the appealing anime eyes, gentle smile, rosy cheeks, warm cream tunic, sage vest, cocoa trousers, and brown boots. Change proportions, camera, and role identity as specified below. This is a miniature adult fantasy diviner in cute chibi proportions, fully clothed.

HEAD REDUCTION — PRIMARY CHANGE
The reference's hair-plus-head dominates the body. Reduce the complete head including hair to approximately ONE THIRD of crown-to-sole height, aiming about three heads tall. Reduce hair width/volume as well, keep soft grouped chestnut locks but remove the tall oversized top tuft. Keep large warm brown eyes with a single tiny highlight, rounded cheeks, tiny smiling mouth, minimal nose and soft thin brows. Do not return to the earlier square-jawed realistic adventurer. Shoulders remain narrower than the head but are not pin-thin; torso and short legs have enough room to read. Short round hands and sturdy boots. Cute, soft, lively, not infantile or muscular. Preserve gentle anime charm despite the smaller head.

ROLE: RESEARCHER / DIVINATION MAGE
A woodland guild mage whose work is searching information through divination. Keep the sage open vest over the beige tunic, add only a short muted-ochre shoulder mantle with a simple round clasp and a small amber crystal orb in a plain belt cradle. Mantle ends above the elbows so relaxed hands remain visible, never a long cape that hides the legs. The orb is modest, around hand size, with muted non-emissive amber facets. It is a magical research tool, not a modern gadget, weapon, magnifying glass, or map. Idle pose has the orb resting at the belt cradle and both hands relaxed. Use largely bilateral outfit construction so a horizontally flipped sprite still makes sense. No lettering, numbered sigils, belt books, bags, staff, hat, armor, extra tools, giant gemstone or floating effects.

CAMERA AND POSE
Front three-quarter sprite facing screen LOWER-LEFT. Eyes, face and chest clearly turned slightly left, with the screen-right cheek/ear and near shoulder giving the depth cue. Gently elevated orthographic camp view: visible hair crown, shoulder tops, and boot tops. Not a straight-on portrait, pure side profile, back view, or extreme overhead angle. Both feet planted at a stable baseline, arms loose and slightly clear of the waist, relaxed cheerful idle. Entire body visible, no walking or casting yet. Ground anchor is midpoint between soles. One pose, no sheet.

PIXEL ART
A deliberately hand-drawn small pixel game sprite with a coherent conceptual 48 by 64 logical cell and a body about 28–32 wide by 54–58 high. These are density guides, not fixed export size. Crisp stepped edges, solid clustered colors, around sixteen to twenty coordinated shades. Warm cream face/tunic, sage/olive vest, ochre mantle accents, chestnut hair, cocoa trousers and brown boots. Soft upper-left neutral lighting contained inside material. Preserve readable face and separate hands/boots at small scale. No noise, detailed weave, hair strands, heavy dithering, smooth vector curves, polished 3D shading, thick sticker outline or glossy portrait finish.

CLEAN TRANSPARENT OUTPUT
The reference's outer brown halo is a defect to REMOVE, not an effect to preserve. Actual transparent PNG: alpha zero outside the crisp stepped figure and inside the arm/leg gaps. NO aura, glow, haze, bloom, blurred edge, soft brown cloud, cast shadow, floor, pedestal, scenery, text, watermark, borders, labels, or alternate poses. Keep the character centered with crown, mantle, hands and soles fully inside clear transparent margins. Exactly one full-body character. Do not include magic emissions; they will be a separate overlay.

## Review criteria

Preserve cute anime face while reducing hair/head to about one-third height. Verify researcher mantle/orb identity, left-facing elevated three-quarter view, clean alpha, and complete silhouette. Candidate approval pending.
