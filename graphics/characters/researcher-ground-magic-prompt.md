# Conspicuous ground casting effect

## Request

Correct awkward walking and move casting effect to a conspicuous ground circle. Generate one candidate per sheet, preserve prior source art. App remains unchanged.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept animation overlay.
Create ONE EFFECTS-ONLY transparent sprite sheet: an eye-catching DIVINATION CASTING CIRCLE ON THE GROUND, FOUR frames arranged in TWO columns by TWO rows, chronological row-major order. This is the conspicuous working-status effect under a tiny cute researcher mage in a dark woodland camp. No character pixels, physical orb or scenery.

VISUAL PRIORITY
The active caster must stand out immediately. A LARGE luminous double-ring magic circle, bright amber-gold with a pale warm-white inner stroke, viewed as a shallow ground-plane ellipse in the camp 2:1 orthographic projection. Main ellipse about 46 logical pixels wide and 23 deep, clearly larger than the two boot footprint. Reference character is only a scale/palette guide, not an object to draw. Circle is centered beneath the caster's feet, never around chest or hands. Keep the center mostly empty/transparent so the ground and boots can remain visible. Use a thin inner ring and stronger segmented outer ring, six floating simple geometric divination glyphs, four short radial accents, plus a handful of tiny rising gold/cyan-white sparks. Include two very small intermittent vertical light streaks at the sides rising about 10 logical pixels above the ellipse; do not draw a continuous column that would cover the caster.

COLOR AND READABILITY
Luminous amber #ffb347 and gold, pale warm-white #fff1b8 highlights, restrained pale turquoise secondary glyph accents to separate the active state from brown ground. Bright concentrated strokes with controlled local soft radiance allowed immediately around the luminous strokes. Beyond that narrow emission falloff, actual alpha zero. Do not turn the whole cell into a yellow haze or a solid glowing disk. The ring/glyph silhouette remains crisp and pixel-clustered, not a smooth vector logo, photographic volumetric effect or muddy dark outline. It should be substantially more visible and festive than the previous tiny quiet orb ring. No red edge noise, black fringe, dark baked background, floor square, terrain texture, flames or smoke.

FOUR LOOP STATES
All cells have identical scale, anchor and ring size. Frame 1 ring and glyphs balanced, two small sparks beginning at lower side positions. Frame 2 segmented outer glyphs advance about a quarter turn while the ellipse remains fixed; white highlights strengthen on alternating segments, sparks rise slightly. Frame 3 glyphs advance again, opposite segments brighten, two short side light streaks at their brightest. Frame 4 glyphs advance again toward the first configuration, old sparks fade as new lower sparks appear. Subtle pulse, readable rotation and spark progression, not four unrelated symbols or a changing circle diameter. Main outer ellipse stays in the SAME cell-local location in every frame. No visual labels.

LAYOUT AND COMPOSITING CONTRACT
Proposed logical cell 96x96, four equal cells 2x2. Ring center at local (48,66), with full ellipse extents roughly x=25..71, y=54..78; rising sparks remain inside x=20..76 and y=34..79. The later compositor anchors that center at the caster's ground-contact midpoint. This asset is drawn behind the character so boots occlude its rear/top edge naturally; never include boots in this sheet. Clear margins on all sides, no crossing cells. Transparent PNG, no checkerboard, backdrop, border, watermark, text, digits, actual letters, recognizable religious marks, star-shaped explosion, character, hand, orb, or other object.

## Review

Candidate only. Verify frame progression, identity/anchor stability, clean alpha and GIF playback before reporting.
