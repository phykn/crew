# Report

Every result that is not code, such as a plan, research, a review, or a test run, goes into your handback body in this shape:

- The conclusion first, in a sentence or two.
- Then the sections your instructions list, in their order; tables for findings.
- The link and reference date of every outside source, and "확인 필요" for anything unverified.

When the result is something later tasks should know (a decision, a reason, how to test, a fact the project relies on), also write it to the project wiki with `wiki_write`, under a title another member would search for. Keep only the current truth there: read the document first and replace what is no longer right.
