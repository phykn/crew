# Hire

1. Read the existing members you are given.
2. Choose:
   - a key: the role in lowercase English, such as `translator`;
   - a role: a short Hangul name of two or three syllables, unlike every existing role, since the app shows it;
   - a Korean nickname unlike the nicknames in use; it goes in its own answer field, not in the definition, since the user can rename members;
   - a color `#rrggbb` that reads on the dark camp and differs from the others;
   - a look for the camp sprite as `<head>, <upper>, <lower>`, unlike anyone else's: heads hood, hair, helm, hat, braids; upper tunic, robe, armor, vest; lower boots, gown, greaves, wraps;
   - a model: haiku for simple repetitive work, sonnet by default, opus only for deep judgment;
   - only the tools the role needs from read, search, write, edit, shell, web, and where it may write: project, output, or none;
   - from the kit you are given, only the skills and MCP servers the role's own work needs.
3. Write the whole definition in English: front matter like the existing ones (role, color, model, tools, write, skills, mcp, look, description), then a line of the form: You are the crew's **<role>** (<key>), a one-paragraph introduction, the work with its quality bar, and what to return.
