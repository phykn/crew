# Tasks

- Telling **비서** what to do opens a task, pinned as a card above the chat. A plain fix that is not code, such as a typo or a wording, 비서 makes at once. The card shows the step under way and whether an answer is needed, with **중단**, **재개**, and **로그**.
- When an answer is needed, an answer card hangs under 비서's message; the choice applies to that task only.
- The **로그** tab lists each line as its time to the second, a mark for what became of it (done, failed, waiting or sent back, at work), and who did what; clicking a line opens the changed files, review points, and test output. Usage by role and model is at the top.
- After the app restarts, unfinished tasks are left **중단됨**; "이어서 해줘" continues from the steps not yet done.
- Typing `/` in the input lists the commands: arrows pick one, Tab fills it in, Enter runs it. `/clear`, like **새 세션**, opens a new session with the same crew while the old session's tasks go on; `/log`, `/memory`, and `/workflow` open those tabs. **중지** next to the input stops 비서's answer under way.
